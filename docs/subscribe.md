# how to subscribe (not import) in your calendar

## 📅 apple calendar (mac/iphone/ipad)

1. open apple calendar.
2. go to file > new calendar subscription.
3. paste the google calendar ical url.
4. click subscribe and set update frequency to every hour (or as needed).

## 📅 outlook (web & desktop)

1. go to [outlook.com](https://outlook.com/) and open the calendar.
2. click add calendar > subscribe from web.
3. paste the google calendar ical url.
4. click import, and it will sync automatically.

## 📅 other calendar apps (android, thunderbird, etc.)

many apps (like thunderbird, zoho, and android apps like business calendar) allow subscribing to an ical url instead of importing it.
look for "add internet calendar" or "subscribe via url."
